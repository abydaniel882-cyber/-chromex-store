## Payment / fulfilment blockers
- [ ] A legal payment method/account with guardian support, if required
- [ ] Confirm payment is received before paying Merch Factory
- [ ] Confirm final supplier price, shipping, taxes, and production details for each item
- [ ] Place and review a test enquiry/order workflow before accepting paid orders
- [ ] Confirm dispatch, tracking, cancellation, defect, and return/RTO policies
- [ ] Set clear customer-facing terms, privacy notice, and delivery estimates
- [ ] Verify custom domain and HTTPS

## Launch rule
Do not advertise the site as accepting orders until payment collection, supplier fulfilment, pricing, contact handling, policies, and domain are tested end-to-end. Until then, the site is a preview/enquiry page only.
