Delivery & policy preview

CHROMEX rules are summarized here for transparency. Final details are confirmed per enquiry before payment.

Production & delivery

CHROMEX's current FAQ estimates 2–4 business days to dispatch for standard orders. Transit time varies by PIN and courier. A final delivery estimate is shared before payment.

Defects / print errors

CHROMEX states verified manufacturing or print defects may qualify for replacement/reprint, with proof and claim within the stated time window. Unboxing video and photos are strongly recommended.

RTO / refused parcel

Refused or failed deliveries may create forward and return shipping costs. We cannot offer COD at launch. Please confirm address and phone carefully.
